TacOps Survival Guide

How to craft every gun, round, attachment and gadget in survival, what the materials are, and how to keep your guns working.

Start here

Everything in TacOps is made on an ordinary crafting table from vanilla materials. No new ores, no new mobs.

Iron first

Nearly every recipe needs iron. Gunpowder comes next, so don't run from creepers.

Recipe book

Recipes show up in your recipe book as you pick up their materials. You can craft any of them as soon as you have the ingredients.

Magazines

Guns reload from magazines in your inventory or offhand. No magazine, no reload. New guns come loaded, and the counter beside your hotbar shows what's left.

Keep them clean

Guns wear with every shot and start jamming when worn. Craft a few cleaning kits.

Weapons

Every gun comes with a full magazine when you craft it.

Tactical Rifle

Rifle · full-auto

  • Iron Ingot×3
  • Gunpowder×1
  • Tripwire Hook×1
  • Any Planks×1
≈ 3.5 iron ingots
  • 30-round magazine, 45 with an Extended Magazine.
  • 6 damage a round. Built-in 3x scope when you aim.
  • Loads from Rifle Magazines. 1200 durability.

In your recipe book once you have: Iron Ingot

Tactical Pistol

Sidearm · semi-auto

  • Gunpowder×1
  • Tripwire Hook×1
≈ 2.5 iron ingots

  • 6 damage a round, iron sights.
  • One-handed: you can fire it with a Ballistic Shield in your other hand. 600 durability.

In your recipe book once you have: Iron Ingot

Tactical Shotgun

Shotgun · pump action

  • Redstone Dust×1

≈ 3.5 iron ingots
  • 8 pellets a shot, 3.5 damage each. 6 shells, 9 with an Extended Magazine.
  • Loads one shell at a time: fire or aim to stop loading and shoot what's in.
  • Blasts open wooden doors and trapdoors within 2 blocks. 600 durability.

In your recipe book once you have: Iron Ingot

M24 Sniper Rifle

Sniper rifle · bolt action

  • Any Planks×1
  • Gunpowder×1
  • Tripwire Hook×1

  • 8x scope, dead on when aimed and wild from the hip. Hold Sprint while scoped to hold your breath and steady the sway.
  • Rounds drop over distance: the reticle's marks show where to aim at 100, 200 and 300 blocks.
  • Players it's aimed at see the scope glint, from up to 256 blocks. 800 durability.

In your recipe book once you have: Spyglass

M80 Zolja

Rocket launcher · single shot

  • Copper Ingot×1
  • Redstone Dust×1
  • Gunpowder×1
  • TNT×1
≈ 5 iron ingots
  • 35 damage on a direct hit, then an explosion that blows through doors and glass.
  • Backblast: anyone within 3 blocks behind you gets burned, and a wall right behind you hurts you.
  • Comes loaded. Reload with a 64mm Rocket. Takes no attachments.

In your recipe book once you have: TNT

Ammo

Press R to reload, or pull the trigger on an empty gun. A reload uses up one magazine however many rounds were left, so don't top up after a couple of shots. The counter beside your hotbar shows the rounds in the gun and the magazines you have left.

Attachments

Each gun has four slots: muzzle, magazine, optic and underbarrel, one attachment in each. The laser and the flashlight share the underbarrel slot, and the suppressor and the chokes share the muzzle.

Crafting grid

Put the gun and the attachment in any crafting grid.

Inventory

Pick up the attachment and right-click it onto the gun. Right-click the gun with an empty hand to take the last one off.

Gunsmith Workbench

Lay the gun on the bench to fit and remove attachments and see the stats change.

Gear

Armour, the shield and healing.

Plate Carrier

Chest armour

  • Leather×4
  • Iron Ingot×3
  • Any Wool×1
≈ 3 iron ingots
  • Its plates stop 60% of gunshot damage to the upper body, and of grenade and claymore shrapnel.
  • Head and leg hits miss the plates. 500 durability, repair with iron ingots.

Ballistic Helmet

Helmet

  • Iron Ingot×5
  • Leather×1
≈ 5 iron ingots
  • Its shell stops half the damage of gunshots to the head: a sniper headshot leaves you standing.
  • Against anything else, as good as an iron helmet. 400 durability, repair with iron ingots.
  • Mount Night Vision Goggles on it: craft the two together, or right-click the goggles onto it. N switches them on as usual.

In your recipe book once you have: Iron Ingot

Upkeep and training

Every shot costs a gun one point of durability. Guns never break, but under 20% durability each pull of the trigger has a 5% chance to jam, and a wrench shows beside the ammo counter. Press R to rack the slide and clear a jam; it uses no ammo. A Gun Cleaning Kit puts the durability back.

Gun Cleaning Kit

Upkeep

  • Iron Ingot×1
  • Any Wool×1
  • Paper×1
  • Copper Ingot×1
Shapeless: any layout≈ 1 iron ingot
  • 4 uses. Each restores half a gun's durability and clears a jam.
  • Craft it together with the gun, or hold it in your offhand and sneak + right-click with the gun.

In your recipe book once you have: Tactical Pistol or Tactical Rifle

Upkeep · block

Controls

All TacOps keys can be changed in Options → Controls, under TacOps.

KeyWhat it does
Left clickFire
Right clickAim down the sights, raise the shield, inject a stimpack, or pull a grenade's pin (release to throw)
RReload, or rack the slide to clear a jam
Q / ELean left / right around corners
FWeapon flashlight on or off, while holding a gun with one. Otherwise it swaps hands as usual
NNight vision goggles on or off, worn or mounted on a Ballistic Helmet
Sprint (Ctrl)Hold while looking through the sniper's scope to hold your breath and steady your aim
Sneak + right clickClean the gun in your hand with a kit in your offhand, or defuse a claymore. Sneak while throwing to lob a grenade

Server rules

For server owners and map makers. Set with /gamerule, for example /gamerule tactical_shooter:explosives_break_blocks false, or when creating a world, in the Edit Game Rules screen under TacOps.

RuleDefaultWhat it does
tactical_shooter:explosives_break_blockstrueSet to false, frag grenades, C4 and rockets still hurt and knock back, but break no blocks.
tactical_shooter:shotgun_breachingtrueSet to false, shotgun blasts no longer shatter doors, trapdoors, fences, gates or glass, with or without a Breaching Choke.
tactical_shooter:gun_weartrueSet to false, shots don't wear guns out and guns never jam.
tactical_shooter:infinite_ammofalseSet to true, reloading uses no magazines, shells or rockets, for everyone. Guns still need reloading.
tactical_shooter:gun_damage_multiplier1.0From 0 to 10. Scales the damage of bullets, shotgun pellets and rocket direct hits. Explosions aren't scaled.

TacOps weapons also follow vanilla's own settings. With pvp off, or between teammates on a team with friendly fire off, other players' guns, explosives and flashbangs can't hurt, throw or blind you, and claymores ignore you.
